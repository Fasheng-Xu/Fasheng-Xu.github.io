Example / uncertain demand

How much
is enough?

Order costs $2 per unit. Each unit of unfilled demand adds a $5 penalty. Unsold units have no recovery value. Every round starts fresh.

Cost = $2 × order
+ $5 × unfilled demand

Instructor note & fixed demand sequence

Demand is 30, then 70, then 50 units. Every reset repeats this sequence. This simplified model isolates over-ordering and shortage costs; it is a deterministic classroom illustration, not a forecast or an AI calculation.

A worked round

Order 40 units. Demand is 30. Ten units remain and none are unfilled. Cost is $80.

Discussion: what information would justify ordering more next time?
