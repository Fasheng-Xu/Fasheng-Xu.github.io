Teaching

Teach the course
you want to teach.

Your students, your learning goals, your way of teaching. Connect the materials you have to the understanding you want them to leave with.

Give the materials a through line.

Develop lectures, PowerPoint slides, worked examples and assignments. Shape scattered notes into a coherent teaching package or textbook draft, with a clear sequence and consistent terminology.

Redesign for a classroom with AI.

Decide what students should practice themselves and where AI belongs. Develop activities, assessments and rubrics that ask them to explain and defend their reasoning.

Let students make the decision.

Create a custom simulation game with roles, decision rounds and instructor controls. Use the debrief to connect what happened in the room to the ideas in the course.
