What is in a Readiness Review?+

A prioritized report, evidence for the findings, and a revision plan you can review. The automated analytical workflow works from the LaTeX source and available figure code.

Readiness and fit
A qualitative assessment, including “not yet” or “consider another venue”. Journal and department fit, contribution, and concerns ranked by severity.
Technical checks
Assumptions, derivations, comparative statics and figures. Each finding identifies its location, evidence, confidence and the limits of the check.
Citations and submission rules
Bibliographic checks, support for key claims, format requirements and possible backup venues. Always verify the current journal rules.
Actionable revision files
A browser-readable report, AI-use disclosure text and a record of review stages. When suggested edits apply, marked-up LaTeX preserves the originals and a diff shows the changes.

For example, a Management Science submission is considered against the department you name. A clean audit does not certify correctness or predict acceptance.

What can I use on this website today?

The project brief tool helps you organize a research, teaching or funding project. It runs locally, makes no AI request and does not submit a service request. The current automated research engine accepts analytical LaTeX projects. Empirical work, teaching and funding projects require an agreed scope and workflow.

What does “personal” mean?

Your methods, target journals, writing preferences, students and learning goals should shape the work. The aim is context you can inspect, correct and choose to reuse. This website does not create an account or remember your brief after you leave; download a copy to keep it.

How should I handle confidential materials?

Use the brief to describe your materials, rather than pasting documents. Before sharing work for a service, agree the access and retention terms, including provider retention. Human access to a manuscript requires your permission. Research support is for authors and authorized collaborators; manuscripts received in confidence for peer review are not accepted.

Who verifies the work and makes the decisions?

You do. Check proposed revisions, citations, solutions and teaching materials before use. Feedback carries uncertainty and does not certify correctness or predict publication. Follow the AI-disclosure rules of your journal, funder and institution. Co-Professor is independent and is not affiliated with any named journal or institution.

How do scope, pricing and access work?

Scope and pricing need to be agreed for each service. No paid plan or checkout is offered on this page. Access to AI services follows the regions supported by the providers used for the workflow.
